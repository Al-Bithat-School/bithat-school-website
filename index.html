FIND YOUR CAMPUS

Our schools & colleges

Three main campuses in Rajoa Sadat with full profiles, plus other Trust-listed schools across Pakistan. Open official links for the latest information.

BOYS
⌖ Rajoa Sadat, Chiniot

Bi’that Boys Higher Secondary School

Modern education (primary to higher secondary) with Quranic studies. Fully equipped science & computer labs, hostel from Class 6 onward, scholarships, sports and Faisalabad Board affiliation since 2007. Principal: Sir Syed Kazim Raza Zaidi. Currently ~215 students, 22 teachers.

View official profile ↗
GIRLS
⌖ Rajoa Sadat, Chiniot

Bi’that Girls Higher Secondary School — Fatima Campus

Founded 10 February 2012. Primary to higher secondary in a pure environment: modern education with Quran and Islamic teachings. Hostel from Class 6 onward, scholarships, sports and Faisalabad Board affiliation. Principal: Mam Kaneez Asmah.

View official profile ↗
EARLY YEARS
⌖ Rajoa Sadat, Chiniot

Bi’that Kids Campus

Nursery to Grade 2 for boys and girls. Modern basics with Quran and Islamic values. Congregational Zuhr & Asr, Islamic etiquette, teacher training and religious occasions. No board affiliation; no hostel. Principal: Mam Maimoona Batool.
